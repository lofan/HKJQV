Canadian Natural Wonders

Moraine Lake

Glacially-fed lake in Banff National Park, famous for its vivid turquoise waters and surrounding Valley of the Ten Peaks.

UNESCO World Heritage Site View Image Source (Wikimedia)

Bay of Fundy

Known for having the highest tidal range in the world, dramatic coastal rock formations, and rich marine biodiversity.

UNESCO: Joggins Fossil Cliffs UNESCO Biosphere Reserve View Image Source (Wikimedia)

Spotted Lake

A endorheic alkali lake in Osoyoos, British Columbia, known for its rich mineral deposits that form distinct colorful spots in summer.

Not a UNESCO Site (Culturally protected by Syilx Nation) View Image Source (Wikimedia)

Iceberg Alley

An area off the coast of Newfoundland & Labrador where massive icebergs drift down from the Arctic each spring and summer.

UNESCO: Gros Morne National Park View Image Source (Wikimedia)
