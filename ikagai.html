Ikigai

Ikigai (生き甲斐) is a Japanese concept that translates to "a reason for being" or "the thing that gets you up in the morning."「生之意義」 它代表了日本人的一種信念： 每個人都有自己的生之意義 也許只是一些微不足道的小事 但這些小事就是你生活的動力 也是使你每天早上醒來充滿幹勁的理由 It is the intersection where your passion and talents meet the world's needs and practical realities. In Western circles, it is often visualized as a Venn diagram with four overlapping circles:

The Four Pillars

What you love: Your passions and what brings you joy. What you are good at: Your skills, strengths, and natural talents. What the world needs: The value you can provide to others or your community. What you can be paid for: Sustainability and financial viability.
