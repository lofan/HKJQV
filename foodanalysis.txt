📸 Copy & paste template

Please analyze this food product from the nutrition label in the attached photo.

1. Serving calculation

* Identify the serving size.
* Calculate the nutrition for the entire package/can/container.
* Show calories, protein, total carbohydrates, fibre, sugar, fat, saturated fat, sodium, potassium and other important nutrients.

2. Glycemic Impact
Analyze the likely blood-glucose impact:

* Estimate available/net carbohydrates.
* Explain the likely GI range, if a reliable estimate is possible.
* Estimate Glycemic Load (GL) per serving and for the whole package.
* Classify the impact as 🟢 Low / 🟡 Moderate / 🔴 High.
* Explain how fibre, protein, fat, resistant starch and added sugar affect the result.
* Clearly distinguish known label data from estimates.

3. Ingredients

* Analyze the ingredient list.
* Identify added sugar, refined carbohydrates, sweeteners and highly processed ingredients.
* Identify potentially beneficial ingredients such as legumes, whole grains, nuts, seeds or vegetables.
* Highlight anything I should be cautious about.

4. Health assessment
Rate the product from 1–10 for:

* 🩸 Blood-glucose friendliness
* 🌾 Fibre
* 💪 Protein
    🔥 Calorie efficiency
    🧂 Sodium
* 🥗 Overall nutritional quality

5. Portion recommendation
Tell me what portion would be reasonable if my goal is:

* maintaining stable blood sugar
* weight management
* getting enough protein and fibre

6. Food pairing
Suggest the best foods to eat with it to reduce the glucose spike and improve satiety.

7. Bottom line
Give me a simple verdict:
🟢 Eat regularly / 🟡 Eat in moderation / 🔴 Better alternatives available

If the label information is unclear or the product weight is missing, tell me exactly what information you need rather than guessing.

⭐ Even shorter version for everyday use

If you want something you can use quickly with every food photo:

Analyze this food label. Calculate nutrition for the whole package, estimate available carbs and GI/GL, assess likely blood-glucose impact, fibre/protein/sugar/sodium, analyze ingredients, recommend an appropriate portion and food pairings, and give me a 🟢🟡🔴 overall health verdict. Clearly separate actual label data from estimates.

📊 For comparing several foods

You can also say:

Compare these food products for blood-sugar control and overall nutrition. Normalize the comparison by 100g and by a realistic serving. Compare calories, protein, fibre, total carbs, available carbs, sugar, sodium, estimated GI/GL and nutrient density. Rank them from best to worst and explain which one you would choose and why.

This template will work particularly well for canned beans, bread, cereal, oatmeal, rice, pasta, yogurt, breakfast foods, snacks and canned foods.
