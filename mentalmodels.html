Farnam Street 心智模型全景圖譜 (Latticework Guide)

本指南完整收錄並深度拆解 Farnam Street (fs.blog) 提出的全部心智模型。結合廣東話實戰評析與 English 精確定義，構建跨學科的思考格柵（Latticework），幫助你在複雜變化的世界中做出高品質決策。

🌐 參考來源: https://fs.blog/mental-models/

1. General Thinking Tools (通用思考工具)

💡 First Principles Thinking (第一性原理思考)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

核心定義： 剝離所有傳統假設同類比（Reasoning by Analogy），將問題拆解至最底層、無法再被質疑嘅物理或事實真相（Fundamental Truths），然後再由零開始重新建構。

實戰應用： 唔好問「人哋係點做？」，而係問「呢件事嘅物理/經濟底限係咩？」。例如設計新產品時，直接計算原料與能量嘅物理極限成本，而非參考現有市場產品嘅定價。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: Deconstructing a problem to its most foundational, undeniable truths, and reasoning up from there rather than reasoning by analogy.

Key Insight: Removes legacy assumptions, prevents groupthink, and enables ground-up innovation rather than incremental tweaking.

🔄 Inversion (逆向思考)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

核心定義： 查理·芒格名言：「Invert, always invert（反過來想，總是反過來想）。」唔好問「點樣成功」，而係問「點樣會必定失敗」，然後全力避開所有失敗誘因。

實戰應用： 執行專案前進行 Pre-mortem（事前剖析）：假定一年後專案徹底失敗，條列出所有致命原因（現金流斷裂、市場不對位、團隊內耗），並提前建立防範機制。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: Approaching problems backward by focusing on what you want to avoid rather than only what you want to achieve.

Key Insight: Avoiding systematic failure modes is often much easier and more effective than chasing perfection.

🌊 Second-Order Thinking (二階思考)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

核心定義： 追問 "And then what?（然後呢？）"。考慮長遠、連鎖反應（Cascading Downstream Effects）同非預期後果（Unintended Consequences），而非只看眼前直覺結果。

實戰應用： 短期降價促銷可以帶來即時銷量（一階），但長期會拉低品牌定位、引發價格戰同損害毛利（二階/三階）。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: Evaluating the long-term, cascading downstream consequences of a decision beyond immediate, first-order effects.

Key Insight: Extraordinary outcomes come from mastering the second and third-order effects that others overlook.

🗡️ Occam’s Razor (奧咸剃刀)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

「如無必要，勿增實體。」當有多個解釋或假設可以解釋同一個現象時，假設最少、最簡單嘅嗰個通常係正確嘅。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: Among competing hypotheses, the one with the fewest assumptions is usually the correct one.

🪒 Hanlon’s Razor (漢隆剃刀)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

「切勿將能以愚蠢或疏忽解釋嘅事，歸咎於惡意。」能有效減少人際溝通中嘅陰謀論與情感內耗。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: Never attribute to malice that which is adequately explained by carelessness, stupidity, or misunderstanding.

🗺️ Map is Not the Territory (地圖不等於疆域)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

模型、圖表、財務報表都只係對現實嘅簡化抽象（Representation），絕對唔等同於現實本身。永遠要警惕模型忽視咗嘅細節與極端狀況。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: Models and abstraction tools simplify reality and always leave out nuance; never mistake the simplified model for reality itself.

🧪 Thought Experiments (思想實驗)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

利用有結構嘅想像力，在腦海中推演極端邊界條件同極限狀況（例如：愛因斯坦想像跟著光速飛行），測試假設嘅有效性。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: Structured mental exercises used to test hypotheses, explore boundary conditions, and analyze scenarios physically impossible to test.

⭕ Circle of Competence (能力圈)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

清楚劃出自己真正精通嘅領域邊界，只喺圈內做重大決策；同時坦誠面對圈外嘅盲點，唔好將「運氣」誤認為「能力」。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: Identifying and operating strictly within areas where you have genuine deep understanding, while being aware of your blind spots.

🎲 Probabilistic Thinking (概率思維)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

放棄黑白二元論（絕對成功或絕對失敗），改用概率估算（Expected Value / 期望值）評估決策。重視貝葉斯更新（Bayesian Updating），根據新數據不斷修正機率。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: Estimating the likelihood of outcomes using probability and basic statistics rather than binary thinking (certainty vs. impossibility).

2. Physics, Chemistry, and Biology (硬科學模型)

🌌 Relativity (相對論)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

觀察者嘅參考系（Frame of Reference）會決定其觀測結果。在商業同談判中，不同持份者因立場與利益不同，對同一事實嘅解讀會有天壤之別。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: An observer’s frame of reference fundamentally alters how an event or measurement is perceived.

⚛️ Thermodynamics & Entropy (熱力學與熵增)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

封閉系統必然走向混亂同衰退（熵增）。組織、企業與個人習慣如果不持續注入外部能量（創新、優化、反思），就會自然走向混亂同官僚化。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: Closed systems naturally degenerate into disorder (entropy) over time unless external energy is continuously applied.

🧱 Inertia & Momentum (慣性與動量)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

物體（以及組織或個人習慣）會維持原有運動狀態，直到外力作用。改變大型組織嘅方向需要極大阻力，但一旦建立動量，亦具備極強貫性。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: Systems tend to keep doing what they are already doing unless acted upon by a strong external force.

⚡ Activation Energy (活化能)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

啟動化學反應或新行為所需嘅最低能量門檻。欲培養新習慣，必須降低初期 Activation Energy（如將目標縮小至每日看 1 頁書）。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: The minimum amount of baseline energy required to initiate a reaction or trigger a new behavioral process.

🧪 Catalysts (催化劑)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

能顯著加速反應且自身不被消耗嘅元素。在商業中，極致嘅 UIUX 設計或關鍵技術突破，即係降低客戶轉換門檻嘅催化劑。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: Elements or conditions that significantly speed up a reaction without being consumed in the process.

🏗️ Leverage (槓桿原理)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

阿基米德：「給我一個支點，我可以移動地球。」在合適支點施加微小力量，產生不成比例嘅巨大產出（例如：代碼、媒體、資本槓桿）。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: A small input applied at the right pivot point (fulcrum) produces a disproportionately large output.

🧬 Evolution by Natural Selection (天擇演化)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

變異、選擇、遺傳。適者生存（Survival of the Fittest）唔係指最強壯，而係指最能適應環境變化。商業產品亦需快速叠代測試。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: Adaptation through variation, selection, and retention; traits that best fit the environment survive and multiply.

🌿 Ecosystems (生態系統)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

高度互相依存嘅網絡。系統內單一節點或物種嘅改變，會產生漣漪效應影響整條食物鏈與生態體系。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: Interdependent networks where a change in one node ripples through the entire community.

👑 Co-Evolution & Red Queen Effect (共演化與紅皇后效應)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

「你必須全力奔跑，才能停在原地。」競爭者與捕食者都在升級，停留原地意味著被快速淘汰。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: Organisms must continuously adapt simply to maintain relative fitness in an environment where competitors are also evolving.

3. Systems Thinking (系統思考)

🔁 Feedback Loops (正/負回饋迴路)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

Positive (正回饋)： 自自我強化（Self-reinforcing），引發滾雪球或飛輪效應（複利、網路效應）。

Negative (負回饋)： 自我調節（Balancing），阻礙改變並維持系統穩定與平衡（冷氣機恆溫器）。

English Concept & Insight

Positive Loops: Amplifies changes leading to exponential growth or runaway decline.

Negative Loops: Dampens changes to maintain systemic equilibrium.

🚧 Bottlenecks & Theory of Constraints (瓶頸與限制理論)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

系統整體吞吐量完全由最窄嘅嗰個限制（Bottleneck）決定。喺非瓶頸位置優化，對整體產出嘅改善係零。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: Total throughput is limited by the single narrowest constraint; optimizing non-bottlenecks yields no overall improvement.

🛡️ Margin of Safety (安全邊際)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

預留超額承重力、時間或資本緩衝，用以吸收未知風險、計算失誤及極端黑天鵝事件。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: Building deliberate buffer capacity to absorb unexpected strain, calculation errors, or shocks.

🐜 Emergence (湧現)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

「整體大於部分之和。」簡單個體依循基本規則互動，組合後展現出單個個體完全不具備嘅複雜集體行為（如蟻群、金融市場）。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: Complex properties and behaviors that arise out of a combination of simple individual components.

🛑 Choke Points (咽喉要道/瓶頸點)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

網絡或流量必經之關鍵節點。掌控 Choke Point（如關鍵專利、獨佔渠道）即可控制整個系統嘅流量與價值分配。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: Critical nodes or narrow pathways in a system where flow can easily be controlled, restricted, or blocked.

⚖️ Homeostasis (體內平衡 / 自我調節)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

系統透過內部反饋機制，克服外部環境波動，自發維護內部動態穩定嘅能力。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: Self-regulating mechanisms by which a system maintains internal stability despite external fluctuations.

📐 Non-Linearity & Scale Effects (非線性與規模效應)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

輸入與輸出不成等比例。規模擴大可能帶來規模經濟（Diminishing costs），亦可能觸發規模不經濟（Diseconomies of scale）。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: System inputs do not scale proportionally to outputs; scaling causes non-linear shifts in behavior.

4. Numeracy & Math (數據與數理邏輯)

📈 Compounding (複利效應)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

收益持續再投資所產生嘅指數級增長。時間同一致性係複利發揮威力嘅核心。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: Exponential growth resulting from continuous reinvestment of returns over time.

🎲 Law of Large Numbers (大數法則)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

隨着樣本量增加，樣本平均值會越來越接近總體真實平均值。短期小樣本極易產生隨機偏差。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: As sample size grows, its mean gets closer to the average of the whole population.

📉 Regression to the Mean (均值回歸)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

極端或異常嘅表現之後，通常會跟隨相對溫和、接近平均水平嘅表現。切勿將隨機波動誤認為長期趨勢。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: Extreme or unusual events are typically followed by more average events due to statistical variance.

🎯 Base Rate Fallacy (基本比率謬誤)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

做判斷時忽略背景整體概率（Base Rate），過度關注眼前生動嘅個案細節。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: Overlooking general class statistics (base rates) in favor of specific, vivid details.

🐋 Fat-Tailed Distributions (肥尾分佈 / 冪律)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

極端罕見事件（黑天鵝）貢獻絕大部分影響力。在 Power-law 領域（VC、金融），平均值失去參考意義。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: Environments where rare, extreme events (outliers) account for the vast majority of cumulative impact.

0️⃣ Multiplying by Zero (乘以零)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

連乘鏈條中只要有一個零，總結果即為零。即使營銷與產品極佳，若合規或安全為零，整體歸零。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: A single zero in a chain of multiplication voids the entire result, regardless of how large other numbers are.

📡 Signal vs. Noise (訊號與雜訊)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

區分底層真實趨勢（Signal）與短期無效隨機波動（Noise）。關注過多 Noise 會嚴重損害決策質量。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: Distinguishing underlying structural trends (signal) from short-term random fluctuations (noise).

5. Microeconomics (微觀經濟學模型)

⌛ Opportunity Cost (機會成本)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

做選擇時放棄嘅最高價值替代選項（Next-best alternative）。真正的成本永遠係你放棄咗嘅可能性。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: The value of the next-best alternative foregone when making a choice.

⚖️ Supply and Demand (供求關係)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

價格與數量嘅均衡點由資源稀缺性與買家需求共同決定。自由市場中最基礎嘅定價機制。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: Price and quantity equilibriums determined by good scarcity relative to buyer demand.

🎯 Incentives (誘因機制)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

「給我誘因，我給你結果。」人對獎懲機制嘅反應係高度可預測的。制度設計錯誤會帶來意外後果。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: Human behavior responds predictably to system rewards and punishments.

🤝 Comparative Advantage (比較優勢)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

即使某人在所有領域都具備絕對優勢，專注於「機會成本最低」嘅領域並進行分工合作，仍能實現總效益最大化。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: Producing a good/service at a lower opportunity cost than others, laying the basis for trade.

🔍 Asymmetric Information (資訊不對稱)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

交易一方擁有比另一方更優越嘅資訊（檸檬市場、逆向選擇），導致市場失靈或溢價折扣。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: A situation where one party in a transaction possesses superior information compared to the other.

👔 Principal-Agent Problem (委託人-代理人問題)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

當代理人（Agent）代表委託人（Principal）做決策時，若利益不一致，代理人會優先最大化自身私利。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: Conflicts of interest arising when an agent acts on behalf of a principal with mismatched incentives.

⚓ Sunk Cost Fallacy (沉沒成本謬誤)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

因無法收回嘅過去投入（金錢、時間）而理性失效，繼續執行期望值為負嘅項目。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: Irrationally continuing an endeavor due to unrecoverable past investments.

🐟 Tragedy of the Commons (公地悲劇)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

開放共享資源因個體短期理性過度開採，最終導致整體資源枯竭與系統性崩潰。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: Shared, open-access resources are systematically depleted because short-term individual gains conflict with collective good.

6. Military and War Strategy (軍事與博弈策略)

🏹 Asymmetric Warfare (非對稱戰爭)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

力量極不對等時，弱者放棄傳統正面對抗，利用非常規戰術（靈活性、速度、區域優勢）打擊強者致命弱點。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: Unconventional strategies used by weaker forces to exploit specific vulnerabilities of a stronger opponent.

⚡ OODA Loop ( Observe, Orient, Decide, Act )

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

觀察→定位→決策→行動。勝利關鍵在於「決策循環速度」比對手更快，在對方反應前破壞其戰略部署。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: Fast decision-making cycles created by John Boyd to out-maneuver opponents in rapidly shifting environments.

⚔️ Attrition vs. Maneuver (消耗戰 vs. 機動戰)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

消耗戰拼資源比拼；機動戰透過快速定位與切入關鍵位置，以極小代價癱瘓對手指揮能力。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: Winning by wearing down enemy resources over time vs. disabling enemy capacity through strategic positioning.

🩸 Pyrrhic Victory (皮洛士式勝利)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

代價極其慘重嘅勝利，其消耗足以直接摧毀贏家本身，實質等同於失敗。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: A victory achieved at such a devastating cost that it is functionally equivalent to a defeat.

🎯 Counterforce vs. Countervalue (反軍力打擊 vs. 反價值打擊)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

Counterforce 瞄準對手打擊工具（武器/基礎設施）；Countervalue 瞄準對手核心資產與價值所在。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: Targeting an enemy's operational capabilities/forces directly versus targeting their assets and valuable resources.

🚚 Fronts and Lines of Supply (前線與補給線)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

前線擴張極限受制於後勤補給能力。沒有安全、流暢嘅 Supply Line，再強嘅作戰部隊亦會崩潰。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: The operational reach of any force is strictly constrained by its ability to maintain unblocked supply chains.

7. Human Nature and Judgment (人性與認知偏誤)

👁️ Confirmation Bias (確認偏誤)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

只尋找、相信並記住能印證自身既有立場嘅數據，主動忽視反面證據。反思方法：達爾文法（第一時間寫落反面證據）。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: Searching for, interpreting, and remembering information that confirms pre-existing beliefs while ignoring counter-evidence.

🧠 Availability Heuristic (可得性捷思)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

根據腦海中容易浮現嘅記憶或近期生動事件（鮮明新聞、親身經歷），過度高估該類事件發生嘅真實概率。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: Estimating outcome likelihood based on how easily specific instances come to mind (driven by recency or vividness).

👥 Social Proof (社會認同 / 從眾效應)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

在不確定環境中，傾向觀察並跟隨大多數人嘅行為，作為自身決策嘅依據。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: Looking to the actions and behaviors of others to guide decisions under conditions of uncertainty.

🚢 Survivorship Bias (倖存者偏誤)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

過度關注成功通過篩選機制嘅「倖存者」，忽視未被看見嘅大量失敗案例（如只研究成功企業家，忽視同等努力但破產者）。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: Focusing exclusively on successful outcomes ("survivors") while ignoring failures that did not make it past selection.

⚡ Cognitive Dissonance (認知失調)

廣東話拆解

當新事實與自身既有信念衝突時所產生嘅強烈心理不適，人類傾向透過合理化（Rationalization）自我欺騙以消除失調。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: Mental discomfort experienced when holding conflicting beliefs, typically resolved through self-rationalization.

📉 Loss Aversion (損失厭惡)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

失去某物帶嚟嘅痛苦感，係獲得同等價值物帶嚟快樂感嘅 2 到 2.5 倍。此偏誤導致止蝕極難。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: The psychological impact of a loss is felt roughly twice as intensely as the pleasure of an equivalent gain.

🛋️ Status Quo Bias (現狀偏誤)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

盲目偏好當前狀態，將任何對基準線（Baseline）嘅偏離自動視為潛在損失或高風險。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: A preference for the current state of affairs, treating any deviation as a potential loss.

🔮 Hindsight Bias (後見之明偏誤 / 事後諸葛)

fs.blog/mental-models

廣東話拆解

事件發生後，誤以為自己早就準確預測到結果（「我早就知道會咁啦」），從而阻礙對真正風險與不確定性嘅反思。

English Concept & Insight

Definition: The inclination to view past events as having been predictable before they happened ("I knew it all along").

8. Latticework 綜合實戰應用 (Multi-Disciplinary Synthesis)

格柵思維（Latticework Approach）： 單一心智模型（Man with a Hammer）係危險嘅。要獲得真正嘅智慧，你必須將來自物理、生物、系統、經濟同心理學嘅數十個模型，交織成一個動態嘅格柵網絡。
